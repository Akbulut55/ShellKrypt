# Contributing To ShellKrypt

ShellKrypt is source-available commercial software distributed under the
[`ShellKrypt Source License 1.0`](LICENSE). It is not an open-source project.

## Before Contributing

- For substantial changes, opening an issue or discussion first is recommended
  to confirm scope and direction.
- Do not include confidential information, real credentials, vault data, or
  material that you do not have the right to submit.
- Keep changes focused and follow the repository's engineering and security
  requirements.

Do not open public issues for suspected vulnerabilities involving sensitive
details. Follow the private reporting process in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
